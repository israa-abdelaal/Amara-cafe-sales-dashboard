# Amara Cafe Sales Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard that shows how sales perform across three cafe branches, which products sell best, and how customers pay.

> Data: real sales data from the cafe, shared with permission." 

## Business question

Which branches, products and payment methods drive sales, and how are sales changing over time?

## What the dashboard shows

- Monthly sales trend
- Cash vs cheque split
- Top 5 products by revenue
- Sales by branch, with a branch filter (Mecca Mall, Al Shawqiya, Wadi Al-Ahd)

## Key insights

- Sales grew 1.28% compared with the previous period.
- Wafer Frappe with Chocolate is the top seller (8.89K).
- Al Shawqiya leads branch sales (20K).
